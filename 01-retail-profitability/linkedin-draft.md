My first analytics portfolio case study: retail sales and profitability.

Using clearly labeled synthetic data, I built reproducible Python/SQLite analysis, defined weighted gross margin, and designed a dashboard with a Power BI implementation guide.

The scenario generated CAD 485,415.00 revenue at 22.06% gross margin. I explored how category cost structures and discount bands affect profit—and why margin percentage alone cannot determine a promotion strategy.

The repository includes data, SQL, validation checks, visuals and business recommendations. These are illustrative results, not real retailer performance.
