New portfolio project: customer retention and cohort analysis.

I used original synthetic subscription data to analyze 600 customers across six acquisition cohorts. Month-3 retention was 74.67% (448/600).

The project includes reproducible Python and SQLite, cohort and channel metrics, validation checks, a dashboard visual, and a Power BI implementation guide. A key lesson: compare equally mature cohorts, preserve the acquisition denominator, and avoid interpreting channel associations as causal effects.

These figures are illustrative—not real company performance. No PBIX is included.
