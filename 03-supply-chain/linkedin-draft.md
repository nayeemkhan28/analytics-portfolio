New portfolio case study: supplier delivery performance.

I built a reproducible Python/SQLite analysis of 180 synthetic purchase orders. Overall OTIF was 31.67%. The project distinguishes order-level delivery reliability from unit-weighted fill rate and documents why completed-order reports can miss open-order risk.

Includes full synthetic data, SQL, validation checks, a dashboard visual, business recommendations and a Power BI implementation guide. Figures are illustrative, not real supplier performance. No PBIX is included.
